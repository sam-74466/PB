Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Who we are

Pawarbuildkon is the parent group of Pawar Waterproofing, Pawar Plumbing, Pawar Hardware, Pawar Rental Machineries and CarRescue24, based in Nanded City, Pune, Maharashtra, India. Questions about this policy: pawarwaterproofing86@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, mobile number, the business you chose and your message.
  • Calls, WhatsApp and email: whatever you choose to send us.
  • Analytics, only if you accept cookies: pages visited, approximate location, device and browser details.

We do not ask for payment details or government ID numbers on this website.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry and pass it to the right Pawar business. With your consent, we use analytics to improve the website. We rely on your consent, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Cookies

By default this website sets no cookies. If you click "Accept analytics", Google Analytics sets cookies that measure how the site is used. Your choice is saved in your browser and you can change it any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Who we share it with

Your enquiry goes to the relevant Pawar business. We also use service providers such as our website host, our form service and Google Analytics (only with consent). We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries as long as needed to respond and keep business records, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing pawarwaterproofing86@gmail.com. You can use the same address for any grievance.

Security and children

The site is served over HTTPS. No system is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information through the form. The site is not meant for children under 18.

Other websites

Our business websites and other linked sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
